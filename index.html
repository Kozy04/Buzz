📖 How-To Outreach Guide

Step-by-step instructions for email setup, auto-pilot, and lead scouting

Welcome to Buzz Outreach! ⚡

Buzz turns cold email into warm client conversations by combining live Google Search lead discovery, Gemini 2.5 Flash personalized copywriting, and custom-branded before/after visual mockups.

1

Set Up Direct Email Dispatch

Configure how Buzz sends your emails. If you have a custom domain email on cPanel (e.g. you@yourdomain.com), use Option 1 (cPanel Bridge). If you want to send from Gmail or a Gmail alias, use Option 2 (Google Apps Script).

2

Select or Scout Your Leads

Buzz comes pre-loaded with verified target leads. You can also tap "🔍 AI Scout" to find active local businesses in any Country, State, and LGA (City) in real time using Google Search Grounding.

3

Send 1-by-1 or Run Auto-Pilot

Tap "Draft & Send" on any lead to preview personalized copy and generate a branded visual mockup. Or tap "Select All Pending" and launch the "⚡ Auto-Pilot Campaign" to dispatch personalized emails with attached mockups hands-free in the background!

Option 1: cPanel PHP Bridge (Recommended) ✉️

Directly sends cold emails from your custom domain email address (e.g. you@yourdomain.com) with attached 1280x720 .jpg visual mockups. Works on any cPanel hosting (Namecheap, Hostinger, Bluehost, GoDaddy, VPS, etc.).

Step 1

Get the buzz-send.php Bridge File

Download the production-ready PHP file to your device, or copy its source code:

📥 Download buzz-send.php
Step 2

Upload to cPanel public_html

  1. Log in to your hosting account and open cPanel.
  2. Click File Manager in the Files section.
  3. Double-click to open your public_html folder (this is your website root directory).
  4. Click the Upload icon at the top toolbar, select buzz-send.php, and wait until the progress bar turns green (100%).
💡 Your Bridge URL is: https://yourdomain.com/buzz-send.php (replace yourdomain.com with your actual domain name).
Step 3

Configure & Test inside Buzz

  1. Open Settings (⚙️) in Buzz.
  2. Set Gateway to: Method 1: cPanel PHP Bridge.
  3. In Bridge URL, enter: https://yourdomain.com/buzz-send.php.
  4. In From Email, enter your custom email: e.g. you@yourdomain.com.
  5. In From Sender Name, enter your name: e.g. Korede.
  6. Tap "🧪 Test Direct Dispatch". You will immediately receive a test email with an attached mockup in your inbox!
  7. Tap "✓ Save Profile & Settings".

Option 2: Google Apps Script (Gmail / Alias) 📬

Dispatches emails directly through your authenticated Google Account (either your personal @gmail.com or any custom domain alias configured in Gmail settings). 100% free serverless webhook.

Step 1

Copy Google Apps Script Code

Click below to copy the single-function webhook script to your clipboard:

Step 2

Create a Project on Google

  1. Open script.google.com in a new browser tab.
  2. Click + New project in the top left.
  3. Select all placeholder code in the editor (Ctrl+A), delete it, and paste your copied code.
  4. Click the Save floppy disk icon (or Ctrl+S).
Step 3

Deploy as Web App

  1. In the top right, click Deploy ➔ New deployment.
  2. Click the gear icon next to "Select type" and pick Web app.
  3. Set Execute as: Me (your@gmail.com).
  4. Set Who has access: Anyone.
  5. Click Deploy. Google will ask for permission: click Authorize access, select your account, click Advanced, then click Go to Untitled project (unsafe).
  6. Copy the generated Web app URL (starts with https://script.google.com/macros/s/.../exec).
Step 4

Paste & Save in Buzz

Open Buzz Settings (⚙️), set Gateway Method to Method 2: Google Apps Script Webhook, paste your Web app URL, and tap "🧪 Test Direct Dispatch"!

⚡ Auto-Pilot Bulk Outreach Campaign

Run high-converting, personalized cold outreach campaigns across dozens of prospects simultaneously while you relax.

1

Batch Selecting Leads

In your leads list, check individual prospect cards or tap "Select All Pending" in the top toolbar. A glowing floating action bar will instantly pop up at the bottom showing your selected count.

2

Hands-Free Automated Pipeline

When you click "⚡ Run Auto-Pilot Campaign", Buzz's engine sequentially executes 5 automated actions for every prospect:

  • AI Personalization: Generates unique copy referencing the prospect's real company name, city, and services.
  • Branded Visual Mockup: Dynamically renders a 1280x720 custom before/after graphic.
  • Direct Send: Sends the email and attachment in the background via your configured bridge.
  • Pipeline Advance: Moves lead status to Contacted and schedules follow-up in +3 days.
  • Deliverability Cooldown: Enforces a 2.0s delay between dispatches to keep your sender score at 100%.
3

Live Execution Controls

Watch the real-time progress bar and log items advance from Queued ➔ Sending... ➔ Sent ✓. You can pause or resume at any time.

3

Google Search Grounding & 1-Tap Import

Gemini queries the live Google Search index to discover active websites, partner names, and public emails, crafting a custom hook for each. Tap "✓ Import Discovered Leads" to instantly populate your pipeline!

🎨 Visual Pitch Mockup Generator

Why pitch with plain text when you can show clients exactly how you transform their business?

1

3 Proven Visual Pitch Concepts

  • Before & After Split Screen: Illustrates the chaos of their current manual bottlenecks on the left vs your clean, automated solution on the right.
  • Client Portal / Executive Dashboard: Shows live metrics, extraction speed, and automated client filing queues branded with their company name.
  • Video Demo Preview Card: Creates a dark glassmorphic video player card with a play button customized with their name ("30-Sec Demo for Sarah at Apex").
2

Single Send vs Bulk Auto-Pilot

In single send mode (via Draft & Send), you can preview the generated mockup, download the high-res .jpg, or tap "📎 Insert Image Note" to add a P.S. note pointing to the attachment. In Auto-Pilot mode, mockups are rendered and attached to every email automatically!
