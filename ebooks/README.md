## 🛠️ Step-by-Step Publishing Guide

### 1. Publishing on Amazon KDP (Organic Search Sales)
1. Go to **[kdp.amazon.com](https://kdp.amazon.com)** and log in with your Amazon account.
2. Click **Create** ➔ **Kindle eBook** (and subsequently **Paperback**).
3. Open `kdp_metadata.md` for the respective book:
   * Paste the **Title** and **Subtitle**.
   * Paste the **7 Backend Keywords** into the keyword fields.
   * Select the **3 Browse Categories**.
   * Copy and paste the formatted **HTML Book Description** into the Description box.
4. **Upload Manuscript:**
   * You can convert the `.md` manuscript directly to `.docx` or formatted PDF (via Word, Google Docs, or Pandoc) and upload it to the KDP interior uploader.
5. **Set Pricing:**
   * Set Kindle price to **$9.99** and check the **70% royalty** option.
   * Click **Publish Your Kindle eBook** (review takes 24–72 hours).

### 2. Publishing on Gumroad / Lemon Squeezy (Direct High-Ticket Sales)
1. Create a new product on **Gumroad** or **Lemon Squeezy**.
2. Copy and paste the product headline, subheadline, and body from `gumroad_sales_copy.md`.
3. Set the tier pricing (**$29 Standard**, **$59–$69 Implementation Bundle**, **$129–$149 Agency License**).
4. Attach the manuscript (PDF/EPUB) and bonus toolkits to the product download page.
