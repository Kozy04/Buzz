## 1. Pre-Flight Marketplace Quality Checklist (Crucial)

Framer's curation team rejects roughly 65% of template submissions. To ensure you pass on your first review:

- [x] **No "Lorem Ipsum":** Verified. All copy in `landing_page_copy.md` is realistic, professional B2B SaaS copy.
- [x] **Zero Responsive Breakpoint Bugs:** Test all 3 breakpoints (Desktop 1280px, Tablet 810px, Mobile 390px). Ensure there is no horizontal scrollbar on any device.
- [x] **Framer Native Styles Used:** All colors and fonts are linked to Framer's centralized Color Styles and Text Styles in the Assets panel.
- [x] **Functional CMS Collections:** The `Blog` and `Changelog` CMS collections each have at least 3 populated entries.
- [x] **Component Variants Cleaned Up:** Hover states on buttons, mobile menu drawer, and accordion FAQ items are interactive and smooth.
- [x] **Layer Organization:** Every layer is named cleanly (e.g., `Hero / Heading Stack` instead of `Frame 4109`).
- [x] **Custom Code Components:** `<InteractivePricingCalculator />` and `<CodeSnippetTabs />` render cleanly without console errors.

## 2. Generating Your Remix Link in Framer

1. Open your completed project in Framer.
2. In the top-right corner, click **Share**.
3. Toggle on **Copy Remix Link**.
4. Test the remix link in an incognito browser window to confirm that a new user can duplicate the project into their workspace with one click.

---

## 3. Submitting to the Official Framer Marketplace

1. Navigate to: `framer.com/marketplace/submit` (or via your Framer Dashboard ➔ **Templates** ➔ **Submit a Template**).
2. Fill out the submission form with these exact optimized fields:
   * **Template Name:** `Nexus AI — Intelligent LLM Gateway & SaaS Template`
   * **Category:** `SaaS` (Secondary: `Technology` or `Startup`)
   * **Price:** `$79` (or `$89` if bundling the companion playbook)
   * **Live Demo URL:** `https://nexus-ai.framer.app` (or your custom domain)
   * **Remix URL:** The link generated in Step 2.
   * **Short Tagline:** "High-converting dark mode template for AI startups and developer tools with interactive pricing calculator and full CMS."
   * **Description:** Paste the detailed overview from `landing_page_copy.md`.
   * **Cover & Thumbnail Assets:**
     * Marketplace Thumbnail: `1600px × 1200px` (4:3 ratio) showing the dark obsidian hero with cyan glow and preview of the bento grid.
     * Feature Slides (3–5 images): Showing the Code Tabs, Bento Grid, Pricing Matrix, and Mobile responsive view.
3. Click **Submit for Review**. Review typically takes **3 to 5 business days**.

## 4. Activating the Framer Creator / Partner Program (50% Recurring Affiliate)

Unlike plugins or widgets, **templates qualify for Framer's Creator Partner Program**:

1. Sign up for the Framer Partner Program at: `framer.com/creators` or `framer.partnerstack.com`.
2. Grab your unique affiliate partner referral handle (e.g., `?via=yourname`).
3. Whenever a buyer duplicates your template and upgrades to a paid Framer site plan (Mini, Basic, or Pro) to launch their custom domain, **Framer pays you 50% of their subscription fee for the first 12 months**!
4. **The Impact:** If a buyer launches a $25/month Framer Pro site, you earn an extra **$150/year in passive affiliate income** from that single template purchase!
